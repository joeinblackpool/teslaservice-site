The 10 channels worth following

Links go to each creator's channel or a live YouTube search for their Tesla coverage — pick any video from there.

01

Doug DeMuro Verified

Famous "quirks and features" format — exhaustive, methodical walkthroughs of every button and control. His Tesla playlist alone covers most models over several years.

02

Auto Focus (MKBHD) Verified

Marques Brownlee's car-focused channel. Reviews Tesla software, screens and Autopilot/FSD from a tech-reviewer's lens rather than a traditional car journalist's.

03

Out of Spec Reviews

Kyle Conner's channel is the one to watch for real-world range, charging-curve and road-trip testing — the numbers Tesla's own spec sheet won't show you.

04

Fully Charged Show / Everything Electric

Robert Llewellyn's long-running UK EV show — now the Everything Electric network — with a UK/European lens that's directly relevant to British driving and charging.

05

Bjørn Nyland

Norwegian EV tester known for rigorous, repeatable cold-weather and range tests — genuinely useful if you're weighing up winter range anxiety.

06

Carwow

UK-based, high-production reviews and drag races — a good entry point if you want Tesla covered in a familiar British motoring-show style.

07

Munro Live

Sandy Munro's engineering teardowns go inside the car — literally — comparing build quality and manufacturing decisions against rival EVs.

08

Now You Know

Deep dives on Tesla news, software updates and strategy — good for owners who want context on what an update actually changes.

09

The Fast Lane Car

Practical, unglamorous testing — towing, payload, cold starts — aimed at buyers cross-shopping a Tesla against a normal family car.

10

Ride the Lightning

Owner-perspective daily coverage — useful for the small, everyday questions a formal review never answers.

Adding more embedded videos yourself
