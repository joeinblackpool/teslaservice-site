About TeslaService.co.uk

TeslaService.co.uk is run by Mr Joseph. It exists for one reason: to help all Tesla owners get good service and value for money.

Finding someone trustworthy to service or repair a Tesla in the UK is harder than it should be. Official centres are spread thinly, and many directories are built to rank on Google, not to help drivers. This site brings together 660 official Tesla Service Centres, HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, Tesla-approved bodyshops and independent garages in one place, with plain-English guides to help you choose.

Independent

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Tesla, Inc. Any listing marked "Official Tesla Service Centre" is Tesla-owned; every other listing is an independent business.

How the site is funded

TeslaService.co.uk is self-funded, with no ads. No garage pays to be listed, and no garage pays for a better position. There are no affiliate links.

How listings work

We cross-check accreditation (HEVRA, The Motor Ombudsman's EV Servicing code) where possible, and link to each garage's real, unfiltered reviews rather than showing ratings of our own. Spotted something wrong? Tell us — it gets corrected or removed, not left up.
