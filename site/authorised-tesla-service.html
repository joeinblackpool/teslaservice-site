Authorised Tesla service: Tesla-approved bodyshops

Independent bodyshops that Tesla has approved specifically for accident and bodywork repair on its cars — not full Tesla dealers, but vetted for collision and structural work.

Last checked: 28 September 2026. Garages open, close and change hands — always confirm directly before booking. Spotted a change? Tell us.

What "authorised" means here: these are bodyshops Tesla has approved for accident/bodywork repair specifically — aluminium panel work, structural repair and ADAS calibration after a collision. They are not full Tesla Service Centres and don't handle warranty or software work. Always confirm current approval status and get a written quote before booking.
