Tesla Model Y service and repair: your questions answered

Short, sourced answers to what Model Y owners in the UK ask most: what servicing it needs, what tends to go wrong, what the warranty covers and where to get it fixed.

Last fact-checked: 9 November 2026 by the XKey Developments team. General guidance — always check your own car's details and current official sources. Repair costs vary by car and garage, so we don't quote prices: ask for a written quote.

Servicing

Tesla Model Y servicing

Does a Tesla Model Y need an annual service?

No fixed one. Tesla lists individual maintenance items for the Model Y, each with its own interval, rather than a yearly service1. A yearly check of tyres, brakes and suspension is still a good idea on a heavy car.

What maintenance does a Model Y need, and when?

  • Brake fluid: health check every 4 years, replaced if necessary1. Tesla says not to top it up yourself.
  • Cabin air filter: every 2 years; HEPA and carbon filters, where fitted, every 3 years1.
  • Tyre rotation: every 6,250 miles (10,000 km), or sooner if the tread depth difference between tyres is 1.5 mm or more1.
  • Brake calipers: clean and lubricate every year or 12,500 miles if you drive on roads that are salted in winter1, which covers most of the UK.
  • Wiper blades: replace every year1.

Brake pads, tyres, the low-voltage battery and other wear parts have no fixed interval: they are replaced when worn. See the full Tesla service schedule.

When does a Model Y need an MOT?

Like any car, at 3 years old in England, Scotland and Wales, then every year2; in Northern Ireland the first test is at 4 years. There is no emissions test, but tyres, brakes, suspension, lights and steering are all checked. Check when yours is due and its MOT history on GOV.UK. See our Tesla MOT guide.

Common faults

What goes wrong on a Model Y?

Why does my new Model Y knock or creak over bumps?

On the refreshed (2026) Model Y, Tesla issued service bulletins SB-25-31-005 and SB-26-31-003 in May 2026 for creaking, rattling or knocking from the front at low speed and on rough roads3, 4. The repair replaces the front upper control arm mounts, replaces the front dampers on Premium and Performance versions, and removes the wiper-bracket braces that carried the noise3. Book it with Tesla and quote the bulletin numbers. See suspension noise.

Does the Model 3 control arm creak affect the Model Y?

Tesla's bulletin SB-20-31-006 for front upper control arm creaks mainly covers 2018–2020 Model 3, but also includes some Model Y built before the part was sealed at the factory5. Ask Tesla in the app whether it applies to your VIN.

Why are my Model Y brakes rusty?

Regenerative braking does most of the slowing, so the friction brakes can corrode, especially on salted winter roads. Tesla lists a yearly caliper clean and lubricate where roads are salted1. See seized brakes.

Does my Model Y have a 12V or a 16V battery?

Model Ys built before late 2021 have a 12V lead-acid battery; cars from late 2021, and the refreshed Model Y, have a lithium-ion one (usually about 16V).6 The two types are not interchangeable, so ask the garage to confirm from the VIN before buying one. If the low-voltage battery fails the car may not unlock or drive, even with a full main battery. See our 12V / 16V battery guide.

My Model Y cabin smells musty. What should I do?

Start with the cabin filter: Tesla gives 2 years, or 3 years for HEPA and carbon filters where fitted1. If the smell stays after a new filter, a garage with air-conditioning equipment can check the system. See cabin filter & A/C smell.

Warranty

Model Y warranty

How long is the Model Y battery warranty?

8 years, with at least 70% of battery capacity retained: up to 100,000 miles on the Model Y RWD, and up to 120,000 miles on the Premium Long Range and Performance versions, whichever comes first7.

What does the basic warranty cover?

4 years or 60,000 miles for cars bought after 1 January 2024; older cars keep the terms in force when they were bought7. See our Tesla warranty guide.

Can an independent garage service my Model Y?

Yes, for non-warranty work. Tesla's UK warranty page says you may have the car serviced at a Tesla Service Centre or a non-Tesla service centre, but warranty repairs must be done by Tesla, and Tesla is not responsible for problems caused by improper third-party repair7. In practice an EV specialist can do tyres, brakes, suspension and filters. Keep every invoice. More in our warranty guide.

Repairs

Getting a Model Y repaired

Can I change the Model Y cabin filter myself?

Tesla publishes do-it-yourself guides for the Model Y filters on its service website1. Follow the guide for your exact car, or ask a garage to do it with other checks. See DIY jobs ranked.

Who repairs Model Y accident damage?

Your insurer handles accident claims; Tesla's own body repair centre and Tesla-approved body shops can do the work. A new windscreen needs the cameras recalibrating. See bodywork & accident repair and windscreen calibration.

How do I check a Model Y for recalls?

Enter the registration on GOV.UK's vehicle recall checker8. Recall work and service bulletins are booked with Tesla through the app. If you are buying used, also read our used Tesla checklist.
