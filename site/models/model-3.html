Tesla Model 3 service and repair: your questions answered

Short, sourced answers to what Model 3 owners in the UK ask most: what servicing it needs, what tends to go wrong, what the warranty covers and where to get it fixed.

Last fact-checked: 9 November 2026 by the XKey Developments team. General guidance — always check your own car's details and current official sources. Repair costs vary by car and garage, so we don't quote prices: ask for a written quote.

Servicing

Tesla Model 3 servicing

Does a Tesla Model 3 need an annual service?

No fixed one. There is no oil to change, and Tesla's Model 3 owner's manual lists individual items, each with its own interval, instead of a yearly service1. Many owners still have a yearly check to catch tyre, brake and suspension wear early.

What maintenance does a Model 3 need, and when?

  • Brake fluid: health check every 4 years, replaced if necessary1. Tesla says not to top it up yourself.
  • Cabin air filter: every 2 years1.
  • Tyre rotation: every 6,250 miles (10,000 km), or sooner if the tread depth difference between tyres is 1.5 mm or more1.
  • Brake calipers: clean and lubricate every year or 12,500 miles if you drive on roads that are salted in winter1, which covers most of the UK.
  • Wiper blades: replace every year1.
  • Battery coolant: under most circumstances it does not need replacing for the life of the car1.

Brake pads, tyres, the low-voltage battery and other wear parts have no fixed interval: they are replaced when worn. See the full Tesla service schedule.

When does a Model 3 need an MOT?

Like any car, at 3 years old in England, Scotland and Wales, then every year2; in Northern Ireland the first test is at 4 years. There is no emissions test, but tyres, brakes, suspension, lights and steering are all checked. Check when yours is due and its MOT history on GOV.UK. See our Tesla MOT guide.

Common faults

What goes wrong on a Model 3?

Why do Model 3s fail the MOT?

Mostly tyres. In 2025 tyres were involved in 71.9% of Model 3 initial MOT failures, and were an advisory on 27.7% of all tests3. Suspension was the next most common reason, in 15.3% of initial failures3, and brakes were an advisory on 10.4% of initial tests3. The car is heavy and quick, so check the inside edge of each tyre before the test. See Tesla tyre wear.

Why does my Model 3 creak when I turn the wheel?

On 2018–2020 cars, the classic cause is water getting into the front upper control arm ball joints. Tesla service bulletin SB-20-31-006 covers it, describes it as a noise-only issue, and Tesla contacted owners to reseal or repair the joint free of charge4. Ask Tesla in the app whether it has been done on your car. More in suspension noise.

Why are my Model 3 brakes rusty or grinding?

Regenerative braking does most of the slowing, so the discs and calipers rarely get used and can corrode, especially on salted UK roads. Tesla's manual lists a yearly caliper clean and lubricate where roads are salted1. See seized brakes.

Does my Model 3 have a 12V or a 16V battery?

Model 3s built before late 2021 have a 12V lead-acid battery; cars from late 2021, and the refreshed Model 3, have a lithium-ion one (usually about 16V).5 The two types are not interchangeable, so ask the garage to confirm from the VIN before buying one. If the low-voltage battery fails the car may not unlock or drive, even with a full main battery. See our 12V / 16V battery guide.

My Model 3 door won't open. What do I do?

Pull up the manual release in front of the window switches on the front doors; the rear doors have a cable under a cover in the door pocket. Use it only when needed. A failing low-voltage battery is the usual reason the doors stop responding. See door won't open.

Warranty

Model 3 warranty

How long is the Model 3 battery warranty?

8 years, with at least 70% of battery capacity retained: up to 100,000 miles on the Model 3 RWD, and up to 120,000 miles on the Premium Long Range and Performance versions, whichever comes first6.

What does the basic warranty cover?

Tesla's Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty is 4 years or 60,000 miles for cars bought after 1 January 2024; older cars keep the terms in force when they were bought6. See our Tesla warranty guide for exclusions and how to claim.

Can an independent garage service my Model 3?

Yes, for non-warranty work. Tesla's UK warranty page says you may have the car serviced at a Tesla Service Centre or a non-Tesla service centre, but warranty repairs must be done by Tesla, and Tesla is not responsible for problems caused by improper third-party repair6. In practice an EV specialist can do tyres, brakes, suspension and filters. Keep every invoice. More in our warranty guide.

Repairs

Getting a Model 3 repaired

Does a Model 3 windscreen need calibrating after replacement?

Yes. The driver-assistance cameras sit behind the windscreen and need recalibrating after new glass is fitted. Ask the glass company whether it calibrates in-house. See windscreen & camera calibration.

Who repairs Model 3 accident damage?

Your insurer handles accident claims. Tesla's own body repair centre and Tesla-approved body shops can do the work. See bodywork & accident repair and approved body shops.

How do I check a Model 3 for recalls?

Enter the registration on GOV.UK's vehicle recall checker7. Recall work and service bulletins are booked with Tesla through the app. If you are buying used, also read our used Tesla checklist.
