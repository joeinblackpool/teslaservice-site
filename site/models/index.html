Questions that apply to every Tesla

Do Teslas need servicing?

Yes, but not an annual oil-and-filter service. Each model has a short list of maintenance items with their own intervals2. See the Tesla service schedule and before you book a service.

What goes wrong most on a Tesla?

Tyres, suspension and brakes come up most often, along with the low-voltage battery. Our known issues guide and the symptom checker cover what owners report.

Where can I get a Tesla repaired?

At a Tesla Service Centre, booked in the app, or at an independent EV specialist for work outside the warranty. Use the finder below, or see Tesla repair near any town or village.
