Tesla servicing in Stoke-on-Trent: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Stoke-on-Trent?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 29 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Stoke-on-Trent service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Stoke-on-Trent on our list is Simon Dean Motor Services, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Stoke-on-Trent?

M&M Vehicle Repairs (Fix Auto Stoke) - Longton in Longton, Stoke-on-Trent, about 3 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
