Tesla servicing in Luton: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Luton?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service St Albans, about 11 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Luton service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Luton on our list is Redbourn Auto Solutions, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Luton?

Werrens Prestige in Bedford, about 18 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
