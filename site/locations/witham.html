Tesla servicing in Witham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Witham?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 7 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Witham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Witham on our list is Rallytec, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Witham?

Steer Southend-on-Sea in Rayleigh, about 15 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
