Tesla servicing in Loughborough: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Loughborough?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Nottingham, about 12 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Loughborough service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Loughborough on our list is DC Tyres and Autocentre, about 8 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Loughborough?

Fix Auto Loughborough in Loughborough, about 1 mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
