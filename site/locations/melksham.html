Tesla servicing in Melksham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Melksham?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 23 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Melksham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Melksham on our list is Novuna Vehicle Solutions, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Melksham?

SJ Curtis in Bristol, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
