Tesla servicing in Welwyn Garden City: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Welwyn Garden City?

Yes — Tesla Service St Albans is about 5 miles from central Welwyn Garden City.

Can an independent garage in Welwyn Garden City service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Welwyn Garden City on our list is Redbourn Auto Solutions, about 8 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
