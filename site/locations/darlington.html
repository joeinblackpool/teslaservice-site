Tesla servicing in Darlington: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Darlington?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Newcastle, about 34 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Darlington service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Darlington on our list is EMS Diagnostics Ltd, about 10 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Darlington?

CF Motoring Services (Blaydon) in Blaydon-on-Tyne, about 31 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
