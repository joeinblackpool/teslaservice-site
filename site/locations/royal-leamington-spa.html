Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Royal Leamington Spa?

Can an independent garage in Royal Leamington Spa service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law.
