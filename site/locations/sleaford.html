Tesla servicing in Sleaford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Sleaford?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Lincoln, about 18 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Sleaford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Sleaford on our list is Olive and Nicholson, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Sleaford?

Steer Sleaford in Sleaford, about 2 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
