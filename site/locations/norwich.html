Tesla servicing in Norwich: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Norwich?

Yes — Tesla Service Norwich is about 2 miles from central Norwich.

Can an independent garage in Norwich service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Norwich on our list is A.P. Vehicle Services (East Anglia) LTD, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Norwich?

Steer Prestige Norwich (Pronto) in Norwich, about 2 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
