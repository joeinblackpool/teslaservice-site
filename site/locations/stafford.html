Tesla servicing in Stafford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Stafford?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 14 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Stafford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Stafford on our list is DR Automotive, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Stafford?

Shorade - Cannock in Cannock, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
