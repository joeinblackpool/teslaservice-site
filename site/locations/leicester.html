Tesla servicing in Leicester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Leicester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Nottingham, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Leicester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Leicester on our list is UNR Automotive, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Leicester?

Fix Auto Loughborough in Loughborough, about 11 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
