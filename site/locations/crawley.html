Tesla servicing in Crawley: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Crawley?

Yes — Tesla Service Gatwick is about 1 mile from central Crawley.

Can an independent garage in Crawley service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Crawley on our list is Lindfield Motors, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Crawley?

Steer Horsham in Kingsfold, Horsham, about 6 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
