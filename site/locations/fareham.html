Tesla servicing in Fareham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Fareham?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Winchester, about 16 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Fareham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Fareham on our list is Beresford Auto Service Centre, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Fareham?

Steer Electric Eastleigh in Eastleigh, about 10 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
