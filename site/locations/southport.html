Tesla servicing in Southport: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Southport?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Liverpool, about 17 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Southport service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Southport on our list is The Manic Mechanic Southport Ltd, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Southport?

ARC Accident Repair Blackpool in Blackpool, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
