Tesla servicing in Taunton: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Taunton?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Exeter, about 29 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Taunton service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Taunton on our list is Abacus Vehicle Services, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Taunton?

Steer Exeter in Exeter, about 26 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
