Tesla servicing in Swindon: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Swindon?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 35 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Swindon service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Swindon on our list is Motest UK Ltd, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Swindon?

PanelCraft UK in Swindon, under a mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
