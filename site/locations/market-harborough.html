Tesla servicing in Market Harborough: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Market Harborough?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Northampton, about 17 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Market Harborough service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Market Harborough on our list is Parr Automotive, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Market Harborough?

Castle Coachworks (Northampton) Ltd in Northampton, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
