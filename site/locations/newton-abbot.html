Tesla servicing in Newton Abbot: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Newton Abbot?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Exeter, about 12 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Newton Abbot service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Newton Abbot on our list is AC Garage Services, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Newton Abbot?

Steer Exeter in Exeter, about 15 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
