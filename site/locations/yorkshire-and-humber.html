Tesla service & repair in Yorkshire & Humber

We list 51 Tesla-relevant businesses in Yorkshire & Humber: 2 official Tesla Service Centres, 3 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 22 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 21 other independent Tesla garages and 3 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include Leeds, Sheffield, Huddersfield, Hull, Doncaster, York.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.
