Tesla servicing in Ramsgate: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Ramsgate?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 44 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Ramsgate service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Ramsgate on our list is Broadstairs MoT Centre, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Ramsgate?

Jenkins & Pain in Whitfield, Dover, about 14 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
