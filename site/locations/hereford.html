Tesla servicing in Hereford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hereford?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 37 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Hereford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Hereford on our list is Dinedor Cross Garage, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hereford?

Shorade - Cheltenham in Cheltenham, about 28 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
