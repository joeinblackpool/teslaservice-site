Tesla servicing in Hampshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hampshire?

Yes — Tesla Service Winchester.

Can an independent garage in Hampshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Hampshire on our list is The Village Garage in Bishops Waltham, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hampshire?

Steer Electric Eastleigh in Eastleigh, about 9 miles from the middle of Hampshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
