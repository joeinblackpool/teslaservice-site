Tesla servicing in Powys: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Powys?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 57 miles from the middle of Powys. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Powys service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Powys on our list is S W Maddy and Son in Kington, about 18 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
