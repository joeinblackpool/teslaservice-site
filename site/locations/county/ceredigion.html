Tesla servicing in Ceredigion: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Ceredigion?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 62 miles from the middle of Ceredigion. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Ceredigion service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Ceredigion on our list is EV Wales in Crymych, about 33 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Ceredigion?
