Tesla servicing in Aberdeen: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Aberdeen?

Yes — Tesla Service Aberdeen.

Can an independent garage in Aberdeen service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Aberdeen on our list is HSR Autos in Aberdeen, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
