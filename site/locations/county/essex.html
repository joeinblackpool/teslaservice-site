Tesla servicing in Essex: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Essex?

Yes — Tesla Service Chelmsford.

Can an independent garage in Essex service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Essex on our list is Rallytec in Boreham, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Essex?

Steer Southend-on-Sea in Rayleigh, about 14 miles from the middle of Essex. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
