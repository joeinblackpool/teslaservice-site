Tesla servicing in Staffordshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Staffordshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 17 miles from the middle of Staffordshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Staffordshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Staffordshire on our list is DR Automotive in Stone, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Staffordshire?

M&M Vehicle Repairs (Fix Auto Stoke) - Longton in Longton, about 10 miles from the middle of Staffordshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
