Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Herefordshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 39 miles from the middle of Herefordshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Herefordshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law.
