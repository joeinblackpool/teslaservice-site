Tesla servicing in Hertfordshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hertfordshire?

Yes — Tesla Service St Albans.

Can an independent garage in Hertfordshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Hertfordshire on our list is Redbourn Auto Solutions in Redbourn, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hertfordshire?

Hilton Coachworks in Bishop's Stortford, about 19 miles from the middle of Hertfordshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
