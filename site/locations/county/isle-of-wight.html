Tesla servicing in Isle of Wight: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Isle of Wight?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Winchester, about 27 miles from the middle of Isle of Wight. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Isle of Wight service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Isle of Wight on our list is Beresford Auto Service Centre in Fareham, about 12 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Isle of Wight?

Steer Electric Eastleigh in Eastleigh, about 18 miles from the middle of Isle of Wight. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
