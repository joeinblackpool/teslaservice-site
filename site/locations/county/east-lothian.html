Tesla servicing in East Lothian: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in East Lothian?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Edinburgh, about 25 miles from the middle of East Lothian. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in East Lothian service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of East Lothian on our list is MVT in Edinburgh, about 15 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to East Lothian?

L&J Eaton ARC in Edinburgh, about 16 miles from the middle of East Lothian. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
