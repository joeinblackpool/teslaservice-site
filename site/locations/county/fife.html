Tesla servicing in Fife: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Fife?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Edinburgh, about 23 miles from the middle of Fife. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Fife service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Fife on our list is Electron Garage in Glenrothes, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Fife?

Bodywyse Autocare - Kirkcaldy in Kirkcaldy, about 6 miles from the middle of Fife. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
