Tesla servicing in Conwy: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Conwy?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chester, about 35 miles from the middle of Conwy. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Conwy service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Conwy on our list is Auto Services in St Asaph, about 14 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Conwy?

Autocare Repair Centre in Liverpool, about 37 miles from the middle of Conwy. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
