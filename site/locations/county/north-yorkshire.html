Tesla servicing in North Yorkshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in North Yorkshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Leeds, about 22 miles from the middle of North Yorkshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in North Yorkshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of North Yorkshire on our list is JA Services Ripon Ltd in Ripon, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to North Yorkshire?

Vehicle Bodycare Centre in Leeds, about 22 miles from the middle of North Yorkshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
