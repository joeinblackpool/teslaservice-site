Tesla servicing in Somerset: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Somerset?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 28 miles from the middle of Somerset. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Somerset service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Somerset on our list is Shires Garage Auto Services in Langport, about 8 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Somerset?

Shorade - Bristol in Bristol, about 26 miles from the middle of Somerset. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
