Tesla servicing in Tyne and Wear: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Tyne and Wear?

Yes — Tesla Service Newcastle.

Can an independent garage in Tyne and Wear service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Tyne and Wear on our list is GC Autos in Gateshead, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Tyne and Wear?

CF Motoring Services (Blaydon) in Blaydon-on-Tyne, about 6 miles from the middle of Tyne and Wear. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
