Tesla servicing in Torfaen: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Torfaen?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 12 miles from the middle of Torfaen. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Torfaen service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Torfaen on our list is Bryngwyn Service Station in Blackwood, about 8 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Torfaen?

Fix Auto Caldicot (Mustoes Specialist Vehicle Body Repairs) in Caldicot, about 15 miles from the middle of Torfaen. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
