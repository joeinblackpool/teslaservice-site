Tesla servicing in Norfolk: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Norfolk?

Yes — Tesla Service Norwich.

Can an independent garage in Norfolk service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Norfolk on our list is Swanton Morley Garage Services in Swanton Morley, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Norfolk?

Steer Prestige Norwich (Pronto) in Norwich, about 13 miles from the middle of Norfolk. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
