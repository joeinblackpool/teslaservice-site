Tesla servicing in Wiltshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Wiltshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 32 miles from the middle of Wiltshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Wiltshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Wiltshire on our list is Car and Commercial Servicing Ltd in Devizes, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Wiltshire?

PanelCraft UK in Swindon, about 20 miles from the middle of Wiltshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
