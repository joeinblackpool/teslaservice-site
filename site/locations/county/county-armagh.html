Tesla servicing in County Armagh: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in County Armagh?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Belfast, about 32 miles from the middle of County Armagh. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in County Armagh service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of County Armagh on our list is Drysdale Motors in Dundonald, about 38 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
