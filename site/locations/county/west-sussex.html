Tesla servicing in West Sussex: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in West Sussex?

Yes — Tesla Service Gatwick.

Can an independent garage in West Sussex service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of West Sussex on our list is P D Motors Billingshurst Limited in Billingshurst, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to West Sussex?

Steer Horsham in Kingsfold, about 13 miles from the middle of West Sussex. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
