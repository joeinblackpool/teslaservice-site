Tesla servicing in Rutland: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Rutland?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Nottingham, about 28 miles from the middle of Rutland. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Rutland service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Rutland on our list is Central Autopoint in Corby, about 11 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Rutland?

Steer Prestige Peterborough (Pronto) in Peterborough, about 18 miles from the middle of Rutland. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
