Tesla servicing in Stirling: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Stirling?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Glasgow, about 23 miles from the middle of Stirling. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Stirling service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Stirling on our list is Broomhill Garage in Bonnybridge, about 21 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Stirling?

Mitchell Inglis in Glasgow, about 23 miles from the middle of Stirling. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
