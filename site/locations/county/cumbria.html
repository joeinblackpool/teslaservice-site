Tesla servicing in Cumbria: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Cumbria?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Newcastle, about 66 miles from the middle of Cumbria. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Cumbria service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Cumbria on our list is Tudor Car Services Ltd in Barrow-in-Furness, about 32 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Cumbria?

James Alpe Ltd in Clitheroe, about 52 miles from the middle of Cumbria. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
