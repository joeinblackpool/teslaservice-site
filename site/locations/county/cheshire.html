Tesla servicing in Cheshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Cheshire?

Yes — Tesla Service Chester.

Can an independent garage in Cheshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Cheshire on our list is Ignition Autos in Middlewich, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Cheshire?

Road And Race Restorations in Warrington, about 9 miles from the middle of Cheshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
