Tesla servicing in Berkshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Berkshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service London-Heathrow, about 24 miles from the middle of Berkshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Berkshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Berkshire on our list is Hicks MOT and Service Centre in Woodley, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Berkshire?

Finesse ARC - Bracknell in Bracknell, about 12 miles from the middle of Berkshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
