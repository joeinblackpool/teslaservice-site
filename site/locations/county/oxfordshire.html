Tesla servicing in Oxfordshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Oxfordshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Milton Keynes, about 30 miles from the middle of Oxfordshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Oxfordshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Oxfordshire on our list is Summer Town Garage in Kidlington, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Oxfordshire?

Crash Repair Group - Oxford in Headington, about 5 miles from the middle of Oxfordshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
