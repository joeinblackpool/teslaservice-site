Tesla servicing in Surrey: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Surrey?

Yes — Tesla Service Guildford.

Can an independent garage in Surrey service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Surrey on our list is MMS Technik in , under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Surrey?
