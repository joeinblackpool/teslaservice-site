Tesla servicing in Nottinghamshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Nottinghamshire?

Yes — Tesla Service Nottingham.

Can an independent garage in Nottinghamshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Nottinghamshire on our list is Yardleys Garage in Sutton-in-Ashfield, about 10 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Nottinghamshire?

Alton Cars (Mansfield) in Mansfield, about 7 miles from the middle of Nottinghamshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
