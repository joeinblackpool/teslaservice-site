Tesla servicing in West Yorkshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in West Yorkshire?

Yes — Tesla Service Leeds.

Can an independent garage in West Yorkshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of West Yorkshire on our list is Pro Tech Automotive in Leeds, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to West Yorkshire?

Master Cars (Birstall) in Birstall, under a mile from the middle of West Yorkshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
