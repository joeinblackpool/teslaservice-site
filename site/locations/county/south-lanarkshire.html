Tesla servicing in South Lanarkshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in South Lanarkshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Glasgow, about 25 miles from the middle of South Lanarkshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in South Lanarkshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of South Lanarkshire on our list is Boyd's Motor Engineers Ltd in Carluke, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to South Lanarkshire?
