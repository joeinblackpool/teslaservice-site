Tesla servicing in Clackmannanshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Clackmannanshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Edinburgh, about 20 miles from the middle of Clackmannanshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Clackmannanshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Clackmannanshire on our list is Ewan Lawson Motors in Larbert, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Clackmannanshire?

Eastern Western ARC in Broxburn, about 19 miles from the middle of Clackmannanshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
