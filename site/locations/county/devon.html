Tesla servicing in Devon: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Devon?

Yes — Tesla Service Exeter.

Can an independent garage in Devon service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Devon on our list is JTD Diagnostics in Bovey Tracey, about 11 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Devon?

Steer Exeter in Exeter, about 15 miles from the middle of Devon. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
