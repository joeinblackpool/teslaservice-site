Tesla servicing in Leicestershire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Leicestershire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Nottingham, about 20 miles from the middle of Leicestershire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Leicestershire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Leicestershire on our list is UNR Automotive in Leicester, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Leicestershire?

Fix Auto Loughborough in Loughborough, about 10 miles from the middle of Leicestershire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
