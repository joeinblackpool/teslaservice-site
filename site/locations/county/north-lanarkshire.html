Tesla servicing in North Lanarkshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in North Lanarkshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Glasgow, about 11 miles from the middle of North Lanarkshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in North Lanarkshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of North Lanarkshire on our list is Motorhome Repair Centre in Glasgow, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to North Lanarkshire?

Touch Ups Bodyshop (Glasgow) in Rutherglen, about 11 miles from the middle of North Lanarkshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
