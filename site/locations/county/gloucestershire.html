Tesla servicing in Gloucestershire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Gloucestershire?

Yes — Tesla Service Bristol.

Can an independent garage in Gloucestershire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Gloucestershire on our list is Autocare and Autogas Logistics in Stonehouse, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Gloucestershire?

Steer Cheltenham in Cheltenham, about 9 miles from the middle of Gloucestershire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
