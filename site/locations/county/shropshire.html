Tesla servicing in Shropshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Shropshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 24 miles from the middle of Shropshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Shropshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Shropshire on our list is Priorslee Motor Services Ltd in Telford, about 11 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Shropshire?

Doseley Body and Paint Centre in Telford, about 9 miles from the middle of Shropshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
