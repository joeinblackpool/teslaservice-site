Tesla servicing in Kent: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Kent?

Yes — Tesla Service London-Dartford, Tesla Body Repair Centre Dartford.

Can an independent garage in Kent service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Kent on our list is Britannia MOT & Repair Centre in Aylesford, about 14 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Kent?

Steer Sittingbourne in Sittingbourne, about 11 miles from the middle of Kent. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
