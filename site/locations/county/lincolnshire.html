Tesla servicing in Lincolnshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Lincolnshire?

Yes — Tesla Service Lincoln.

Can an independent garage in Lincolnshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Lincolnshire on our list is D C Baxter Motors Ltd in Ruskington, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Lincolnshire?

Steer Sleaford in Sleaford, about 9 miles from the middle of Lincolnshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
