Tesla servicing in Suffolk: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Suffolk?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Norwich, about 34 miles from the middle of Suffolk. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Suffolk service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Suffolk on our list is NGC Hybrid EV Ltd in Stowmarket, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Suffolk?

Steer Sudbury in Sudbury, about 15 miles from the middle of Suffolk. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
