Tesla servicing in Bedfordshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bedfordshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Milton Keynes, about 13 miles from the middle of Bedfordshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bedfordshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Bedfordshire on our list is Ampthill Vehicle Services Ltd in Ampthill, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bedfordshire?

Werrens Prestige in Bedford, about 8 miles from the middle of Bedfordshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
