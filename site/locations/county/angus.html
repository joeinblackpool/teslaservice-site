Tesla servicing in Angus: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Angus?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Aberdeen, about 41 miles from the middle of Angus. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Angus service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Angus on our list is Cotly Garage in Blairgowrie, about 18 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
