Tesla servicing in Warwickshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Warwickshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Solihull, about 11 miles from the middle of Warwickshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Warwickshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Warwickshire on our list is Ecofix Group in Warwick, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
