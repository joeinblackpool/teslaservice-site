Tesla servicing in Renfrewshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Renfrewshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Glasgow, about 12 miles from the middle of Renfrewshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Renfrewshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Renfrewshire on our list is AMR in Paisley, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Renfrewshire?
