Tesla servicing in Carmarthenshire: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Carmarthenshire?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 50 miles from the middle of Carmarthenshire. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Carmarthenshire service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to the middle of Carmarthenshire on our list is Pontardulais MOT Centre in Pontarddulais, about 14 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Carmarthenshire?

Fix Auto Caldicot (Mustoes Specialist Vehicle Body Repairs) in Caldicot, about 64 miles from the middle of Carmarthenshire. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
