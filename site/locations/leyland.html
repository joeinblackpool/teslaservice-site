Tesla servicing in Leyland: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Leyland?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Liverpool, about 23 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Leyland service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Leyland on our list is Weldbank Garage, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Leyland?

Gillibrand Accident Repair Services in See website, about 10 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
