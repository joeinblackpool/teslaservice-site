Tesla service & repair in Wales

We list 23 Tesla-relevant businesses in Wales: 1 official Tesla Service Centres, 2 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 17 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 2 other independent Tesla garages and 1 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include Cardiff, Newport, Caernarfon, Neath, Caldicot, Pontypridd.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.
