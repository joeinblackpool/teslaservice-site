Tesla servicing in Hitchin: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hitchin?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service St Albans, about 15 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Hitchin service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Hitchin on our list is Auto Service and Tuning, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hitchin?

Werrens Prestige in Bedford, about 14 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
