Tesla servicing in Chichester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chichester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Guildford, about 27 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Chichester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chichester on our list is Fleet Repair Centre, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chichester?

Steer Prestige Chichester (Apollo Prestige) in Chichester, about 1 mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
