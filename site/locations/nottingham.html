Tesla servicing in Nottingham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Nottingham?

Yes — Tesla Service Nottingham is about 2 miles from central Nottingham.

Can an independent garage in Nottingham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Nottingham on our list is Hilltop Service Station, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Nottingham?

Steer Nottingham in Beeston, Nottingham, about 4 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
