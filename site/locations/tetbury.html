Tesla servicing in Tetbury: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Tetbury?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Tetbury service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Tetbury on our list is Car Care Tetbury, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Tetbury?

PanelCraft UK in Swindon, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
