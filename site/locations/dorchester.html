Tesla servicing in Dorchester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Dorchester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bournemouth, about 24 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Dorchester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Dorchester on our list is Adams Garage Dorchester, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Dorchester?

Poole Accident Repair in Poole, about 21 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
