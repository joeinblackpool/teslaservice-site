Tesla servicing in Glasgow: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Glasgow?

Yes — Tesla Service Glasgow is in central Glasgow.

Can an independent garage in Glasgow service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Glasgow on our list is Cam Autos Rutherglen Ltd, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Glasgow?

Touch Ups Bodyshop (Glasgow) in Rutherglen, Glasgow, about 2 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
