Tesla servicing in Perth: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Perth?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Edinburgh, about 32 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Perth service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Perth on our list is Stewart and Smart, about 12 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Perth?

Bodywyse Autocare - Cupar in Cupar, about 18 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
