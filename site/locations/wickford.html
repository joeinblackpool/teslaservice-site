Tesla servicing in Wickford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Wickford?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 11 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Wickford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Wickford on our list is Allstar Vehicle Services, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Wickford?

Steer Southend-on-Sea in Rayleigh, about 3 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
