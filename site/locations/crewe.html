Tesla servicing in Crewe: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Crewe?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chester, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Crewe service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Crewe on our list is Ignition Autos, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Crewe?

M&M Vehicle Repairs (Fix Auto Stoke) - Longton in Longton, Stoke-on-Trent, about 15 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
