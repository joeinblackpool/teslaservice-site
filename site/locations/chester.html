Tesla servicing in Chester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chester?

Yes — Tesla Service Chester is about 1 mile from central Chester.

Can an independent garage in Chester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chester on our list is AJL Car Care Centre Ltd, about 14 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chester?

Road And Race Restorations in Warrington, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
