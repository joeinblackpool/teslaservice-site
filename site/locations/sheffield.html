Tesla servicing in Sheffield: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Sheffield?

Yes — Tesla Service Sheffield is about 5 miles from central Sheffield.

Can an independent garage in Sheffield service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Sheffield on our list is MTA EV Hybrid Specialist, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Sheffield?

Steer Doncaster in Denaby Main, Doncaster, about 11 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
