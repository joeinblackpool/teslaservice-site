Tesla servicing in Oldham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Oldham?

Yes — Tesla Service Manchester Central is about 5 miles from central Oldham.

Can an independent garage in Oldham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Oldham on our list is North Manchester Auto Electrics, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Oldham?

Prestige Repair in Manchester, about 6 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
