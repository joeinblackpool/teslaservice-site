Tesla servicing in Shoreham-by-Sea: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Shoreham-by-Sea?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Gatwick, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Shoreham-by-Sea service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Shoreham-by-Sea on our list is Cedar Garage Ltd, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Shoreham-by-Sea?

Steer Horsham in Kingsfold, Horsham, about 20 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
