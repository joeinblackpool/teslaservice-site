Tesla servicing in Brackley: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Brackley?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Milton Keynes, about 17 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Brackley service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Brackley on our list is QCD Autocentre Brackley, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Brackley?

Crash Repair Group - Bicester in Bicester, about 8 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
