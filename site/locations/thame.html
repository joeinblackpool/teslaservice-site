Tesla servicing in Thame: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Thame?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Milton Keynes, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Thame service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Thame on our list is Chinnor Motors, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Thame?

Crash Repair Group - Thame (Thame Crash Repair Centre) in Thame, under a mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
