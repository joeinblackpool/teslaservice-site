Tesla servicing in Pontefract: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Pontefract?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Leeds, about 11 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Pontefract service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Pontefract on our list is A&J Fleetcare Ltd, about 12 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Pontefract?

Vehicle Bodycare Centre in Leeds, about 10 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
