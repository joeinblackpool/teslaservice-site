Tesla service & repair in London

We list 42 Tesla-relevant businesses in London: 3 official Tesla Service Centres, 5 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 10 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 15 other independent Tesla garages and 9 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include London, Hayes, Romford, Croydon, North London, West Drayton.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.
