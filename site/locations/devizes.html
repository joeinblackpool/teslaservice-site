Tesla servicing in Devizes: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Devizes?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 30 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Devizes service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Devizes on our list is Car and Commercial Servicing Ltd, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Devizes?

PanelCraft UK in Swindon, about 17 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
