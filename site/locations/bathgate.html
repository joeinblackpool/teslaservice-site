Tesla servicing in Bathgate: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bathgate?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Edinburgh, about 10 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bathgate service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bathgate on our list is Bosch Car Service Livingston, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bathgate?

Protek in Bathgate, under a mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
