Tesla servicing in Saffron Walden: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Saffron Walden?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cambridge, about 19 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Saffron Walden service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Saffron Walden on our list is Saffron Autos, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Saffron Walden?

Hilton Coachworks in Bishop's Stortford, about 11 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
