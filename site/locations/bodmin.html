Tesla servicing in Bodmin: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bodmin?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Exeter, about 55 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bodmin service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bodmin on our list is DNA Vehicle Services, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bodmin?

Steer Exeter in Exeter, about 57 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
