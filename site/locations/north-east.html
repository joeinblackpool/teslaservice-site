Tesla service & repair in the North East

We list 24 Tesla-relevant businesses in the North East: 1 official Tesla Service Centres, 1 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 14 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 7 other independent Tesla garages and 1 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include Middlesbrough, Gateshead, Newcastle, Durham, North Shields, N Yorks.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.
