Tesla servicing in Southend-on-Sea: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Southend-on-Sea?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 17 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Southend-on-Sea service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Southend-on-Sea on our list is P Breading Motor Services Limited, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Southend-on-Sea?

Steer Southend-on-Sea in Rayleigh, about 5 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
