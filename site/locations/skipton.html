Tesla servicing in Skipton: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Skipton?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Leeds, about 23 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Skipton service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Skipton on our list is Midgley Motor Cars, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Skipton?

James Alpe Ltd in Clitheroe, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
