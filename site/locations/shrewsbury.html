Tesla servicing in Shrewsbury: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Shrewsbury?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 27 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Shrewsbury service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Shrewsbury on our list is Priorslee Motor Services Ltd, about 13 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Shrewsbury?

Doseley Body and Paint Centre in Telford, about 12 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
