Tesla servicing in South Shields: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in South Shields?

Yes — Tesla Service Newcastle is about 3 miles from central South Shields.

Can an independent garage in South Shields service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to South Shields on our list is Hogg MOT and Service Centre, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to South Shields?

CF Motoring Services (Blaydon) in Blaydon-on-Tyne, about 11 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
