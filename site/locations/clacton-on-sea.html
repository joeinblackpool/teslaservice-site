Tesla servicing in Clacton-on-Sea: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Clacton-on-Sea?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 28 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Clacton-on-Sea service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Clacton-on-Sea on our list is Coastal Car and Leisure Ltd, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Clacton-on-Sea?

Ipswich Accident Repair Centre (IARC) in Martlesham, Ipswich, about 19 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
