Tesla servicing in Guildford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Guildford?

Yes — Tesla Service Guildford is about 2 miles from central Guildford.

Can an independent garage in Guildford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Guildford on our list is CT Cars, about 6 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Guildford?

Steer Prestige Farnham (Steer Luxury South) in Farnham, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
