Tesla servicing in Chelmsford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chelmsford?

Yes — Tesla Service Chelmsford is about 2 miles from central Chelmsford.

Can an independent garage in Chelmsford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chelmsford on our list is City Electric Vehicle Engineering, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chelmsford?

Steer Southend-on-Sea in Rayleigh, about 12 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
