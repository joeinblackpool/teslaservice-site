Tesla servicing in Hastings: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hastings?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Gatwick, about 39 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Hastings service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Hastings on our list is Scants Motor Services, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hastings?

Steer Polegate in Polegate, about 14 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
