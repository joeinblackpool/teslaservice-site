Tesla servicing in Leigh: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Leigh?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Manchester Central, about 14 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Leigh service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Leigh on our list is MK Tuning, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Leigh?

Solution Repair Group in Warrington, about 8 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
