Tesla servicing in Chingford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chingford?

Yes — Tesla Service London-Tottenham is about 4 miles from central Chingford.

Can an independent garage in Chingford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chingford on our list is North London Hybrid EV Garage, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chingford?

EV Bodyshops in See website, about 2 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
