Tesla servicing in Lincoln: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Lincoln?

Yes — Tesla Service Lincoln is about 4 miles from central Lincoln.

Can an independent garage in Lincoln service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Lincoln on our list is Foster and Sims, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.
