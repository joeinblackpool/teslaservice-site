Tesla servicing in Middlewich: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Middlewich?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chester, about 19 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Middlewich service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Middlewich on our list is Ignition Autos, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Middlewich?

Road And Race Restorations in Warrington, about 11 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
