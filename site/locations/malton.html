Tesla servicing in Malton: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Malton?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Leeds, about 39 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Malton service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Malton on our list is Ishy's Autoelectrics, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Malton?

Vehicle Bodycare Centre in Leeds, about 38 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
