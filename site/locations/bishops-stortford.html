Tesla servicing in Bishop's Stortford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bishop's Stortford?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 16 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bishop's Stortford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bishop's Stortford on our list is Grasmere Garage, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bishop's Stortford?

Hilton Coachworks in Bishop's Stortford, under a mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
