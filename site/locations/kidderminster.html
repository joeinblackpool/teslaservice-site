Tesla servicing in Kidderminster: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Kidderminster?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 16 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Kidderminster service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Kidderminster on our list is Motor1 Ltd, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Kidderminster?

Reynolds of Rushock in Rushock, Worcs, about 5 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
