Tesla servicing in Rye: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Rye?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service London-Dartford, about 41 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Rye service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Rye on our list is Skinners of Rye, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Rye?

Steer Polegate in Polegate, about 22 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
