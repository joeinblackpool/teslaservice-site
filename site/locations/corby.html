Tesla servicing in Corby: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Corby?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Northampton, about 20 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Corby service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Corby on our list is Central Autopoint, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Corby?

Castle Coachworks (Northampton) Ltd in Northampton, about 20 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
