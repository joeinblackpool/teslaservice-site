Tesla servicing in Bromley: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bromley?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Body Repair Centre Dartford, about 11 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bromley service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bromley on our list is Central Auto Centre, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bromley?

Finesse ARC - Redhill in Redhill, about 13 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
