Tesla servicing in Peacehaven: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Peacehaven?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Gatwick, about 25 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Peacehaven service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Peacehaven on our list is C R Allen & Sons, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Peacehaven?

Steer Polegate in Polegate, about 12 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
