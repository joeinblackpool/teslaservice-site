Tesla servicing in Gloucester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Gloucester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 28 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Gloucester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Gloucester on our list is Autocare and Autogas Logistics, about 8 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Gloucester?

Steer Cheltenham in Cheltenham, about 8 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
