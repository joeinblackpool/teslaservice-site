Tesla servicing in Bishop Auckland: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bishop Auckland?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Newcastle, about 26 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bishop Auckland service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bishop Auckland on our list is EMS Diagnostics Ltd, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bishop Auckland?

CF Motoring Services (Blaydon) in Blaydon-on-Tyne, about 22 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
