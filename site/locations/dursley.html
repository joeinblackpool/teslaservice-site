Tesla servicing in Dursley: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Dursley?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 16 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Dursley service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Dursley on our list is RS Autotechnik, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Dursley?

Fix Auto Caldicot (Mustoes Specialist Vehicle Body Repairs) in Caldicot, about 18 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
