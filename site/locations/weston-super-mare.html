Tesla servicing in Weston-super-Mare: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Weston-super-Mare?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 15 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Weston-super-Mare service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Weston-super-Mare on our list is AutoBliss Ltd, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Weston-super-Mare?

Shorade - Bristol in Bristol, about 17 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
