Tesla servicing in Harwich: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Harwich?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 32 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Harwich service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Harwich on our list is Ramsey Hill Garage, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Harwich?

Ipswich Accident Repair Centre (IARC) in Martlesham, Ipswich, about 10 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
