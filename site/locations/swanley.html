Tesla servicing in Swanley: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Swanley?

Yes — Tesla Service London-Dartford is about 5 miles from central Swanley.

Can an independent garage in Swanley service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Swanley on our list is Swanley Garage, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Swanley?

Steer Prestige Romford in Romford, about 14 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
