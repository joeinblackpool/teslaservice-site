Tesla servicing in Darwen: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Darwen?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Manchester Central, about 19 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Darwen service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Darwen on our list is Lodge Bank Garage, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Darwen?

Gillibrand Accident Repair Services in See website, about 5 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
