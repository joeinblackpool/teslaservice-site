Tesla service & repair in the West Midlands

We list 57 Tesla-relevant businesses in the West Midlands: 2 official Tesla Service Centres, 9 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 27 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 13 other independent Tesla garages and 6 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include Birmingham, Telford, Worcester, Stoke-on-Trent, Burton-on-Trent, Wolverhampton.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.
