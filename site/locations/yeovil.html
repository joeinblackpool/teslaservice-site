Tesla servicing in Yeovil: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Yeovil?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bournemouth, about 33 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Yeovil service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Yeovil on our list is Robert Cockings Motor Repairs, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Yeovil?

Poole Accident Repair in Poole, about 32 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
