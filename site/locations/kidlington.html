Tesla servicing in Kidlington: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Kidlington?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Milton Keynes, about 27 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Kidlington service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Kidlington on our list is Summer Town Garage, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Kidlington?

Crash Repair Group - Oxford in Headington, Oxford, about 7 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
