Tesla servicing in Clitheroe: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Clitheroe?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Manchester Central, about 29 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Clitheroe service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Clitheroe on our list is Lodge Bank Garage, about 13 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Clitheroe?

James Alpe Ltd in Clitheroe, under a mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
