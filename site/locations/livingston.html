Tesla servicing in Livingston: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Livingston?

Yes — Tesla Service Edinburgh is about 6 miles from central Livingston.

Can an independent garage in Livingston service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Livingston on our list is Bosch Car Service Livingston, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Livingston?

Protek in Bathgate, about 4 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
