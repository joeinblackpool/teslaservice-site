Tesla service & repair in Scotland

We list 44 Tesla-relevant businesses in Scotland: 3 official Tesla Service Centres, 8 Tesla-approved bodyshops, 24 HEVRA-accredited EV specialists, 6 other independent Tesla garages and 3 parts, accessories and cosmetic businesses. Main towns covered include Glasgow, Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Inverness, Paisley, Carluke.

Official Tesla Service Centres handle warranty and recall work. Independent EV specialists are usually cheaper for out-of-warranty servicing, tyres, brakes and suspension. Tesla-approved bodyshops are the ones to use after an accident. Whoever you pick, check these things before booking.

Want them sorted by distance from you? Use the postcode finder. Spotted a closure or a garage we're missing? Tell us.
