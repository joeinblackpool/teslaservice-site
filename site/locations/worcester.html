Tesla servicing in Worcester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Worcester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Solihull, about 22 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Worcester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Worcester on our list is Recharged Automotive, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Worcester?

Reynolds of Rushock in Rushock, Worcs, about 10 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
