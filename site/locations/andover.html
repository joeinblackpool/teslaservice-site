Tesla servicing in Andover: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Andover?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Winchester, about 15 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Andover service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Andover on our list is Knightsbridge Garage, about 15 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Andover?

Steer Electric Eastleigh in Eastleigh, about 21 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
