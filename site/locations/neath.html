Tesla servicing in Neath: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Neath?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 30 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Neath service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Neath on our list is Clive Sinclair Motors, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Neath?

Fix Auto Caldicot (Mustoes Specialist Vehicle Body Repairs) in Caldicot, about 46 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
