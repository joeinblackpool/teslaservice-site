Tesla servicing in Macclesfield: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Macclesfield?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Manchester Central, about 15 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Macclesfield service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Macclesfield on our list is Alan Thompson Motor Engineers, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Macclesfield?

M&M Vehicle Repairs (Fix Auto North Staffs) - Leek in Leek, about 12 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
