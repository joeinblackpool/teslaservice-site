Tesla servicing in Stroud: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Stroud?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 22 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Stroud service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Stroud on our list is Autocare and Autogas Logistics, about 3 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Stroud?

Steer Cheltenham in Cheltenham, about 12 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
