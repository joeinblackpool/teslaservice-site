Tesla servicing in Ely: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Ely?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cambridge, about 12 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Ely service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Ely on our list is Wicken 4x4, under a mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Ely?

Brandon Accident Repair Centre in Brandon, about 16 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
