Tesla servicing in Hartlepool: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Hartlepool?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Newcastle, about 25 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Hartlepool service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Hartlepool on our list is SP Motors NE Ltd, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Hartlepool?

CF Motoring Services (Blaydon) in Blaydon-on-Tyne, about 27 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
