Tesla servicing in Peterborough: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Peterborough?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cambridge, about 24 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Peterborough service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Peterborough on our list is Central Autopoint, about 19 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Peterborough?

Steer Prestige Peterborough (Pronto) in Peterborough, about 1 mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
