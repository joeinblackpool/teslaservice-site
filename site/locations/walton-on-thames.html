Tesla servicing in Walton-on-Thames: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Walton-on-Thames?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service London-Heathrow, about 8 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Walton-on-Thames service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Walton-on-Thames on our list is Elmwood Vehicles Ltd, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Walton-on-Thames?

Sandown Coachworks in Esher, about 3 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
