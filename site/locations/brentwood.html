Tesla servicing in Brentwood: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Brentwood?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Body Repair Centre Dartford, about 12 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Brentwood service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Brentwood on our list is In Front Autos Ltd, about 4 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Brentwood?

Steer Prestige Romford in Romford, about 4 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
