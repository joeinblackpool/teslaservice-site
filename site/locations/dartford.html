Tesla servicing in Dartford: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Dartford?

Yes — Tesla Body Repair Centre Dartford is in central Dartford.

Can an independent garage in Dartford service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Dartford on our list is Swanley Garage, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Dartford?

Steer Prestige Romford in Romford, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
