Tesla servicing in Liverpool: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Liverpool?

Yes — Tesla Service Liverpool is about 2 miles from central Liverpool.

Can an independent garage in Liverpool service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Liverpool on our list is Ashwell Motors, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Liverpool?

Autocare Repair Centre in Liverpool, about 2 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
