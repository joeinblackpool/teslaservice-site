Tesla servicing in Chippenham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chippenham?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Bristol, about 21 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Chippenham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chippenham on our list is Car and Commercial Servicing Ltd, about 10 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chippenham?

SJ Curtis in Bristol, about 15 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
