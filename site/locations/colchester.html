Tesla servicing in Colchester: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Colchester?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chelmsford, about 19 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Colchester service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Colchester on our list is Fairfax Motors Ltd, about 1 mile away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Colchester?

Steer Sudbury in Sudbury, about 13 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
