Tesla servicing in Bridgnorth: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bridgnorth?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Wolverhampton, about 13 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bridgnorth service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bridgnorth on our list is Priorslee Motor Services Ltd, about 10 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bridgnorth?

Doseley Body and Paint Centre in Telford, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
