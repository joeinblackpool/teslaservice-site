Tesla servicing in Solihull: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Solihull?

Yes — Tesla Service Solihull is about 3 miles from central Solihull.

Can an independent garage in Solihull service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Solihull on our list is Harborne Garage Ltd, about 7 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Solihull?

Individual Specialist Cars in Solihull / Birmingham, about 8 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
