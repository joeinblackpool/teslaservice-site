Tesla servicing in Chatham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Chatham?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service London-Dartford, about 13 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Chatham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Chatham on our list is Mr Clutch Autocentre, about 2 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Chatham?

Steer Sittingbourne in Sittingbourne, about 9 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
