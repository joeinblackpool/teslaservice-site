Tesla servicing in Twickenham: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Twickenham?

Yes — Tesla Service London-Park Royal is about 6 miles from central Twickenham.

Can an independent garage in Twickenham service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Twickenham on our list is Goingreen, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Twickenham?

FCW Accident Repair Group in Twickenham, about 1 mile away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
