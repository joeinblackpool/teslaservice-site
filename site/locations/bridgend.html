Tesla servicing in Bridgend: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bridgend?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Cardiff, about 19 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bridgend service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bridgend on our list is Pyle Autocentre Ltd, about 5 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bridgend?

Fix Auto Caldicot (Mustoes Specialist Vehicle Body Repairs) in Caldicot, about 37 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
