Tesla servicing in Bangor: quick answers

Is there a Tesla Service Centre in Bangor?

No. The nearest official Tesla Service Centre is Tesla Service Chester, about 51 miles away. Tesla also offers mobile servicing for many jobs, booked through the Tesla app.

Can an independent garage in Bangor service my Tesla?

Yes. Routine servicing — tyres, brakes, brake fluid, cabin filters, suspension — can be done by a suitably qualified independent without automatically voiding your warranty under UK consumer law. The nearest HEVRA-accredited EV specialist to Bangor on our list is B & K Williams, about 9 miles away. Battery and drive-unit warranty work should go to Tesla.

Where is the nearest Tesla-approved bodyshop to Bangor?

Autocare Repair Centre in Liverpool, about 49 miles away. After an accident, a Tesla-approved bodyshop is usually what your insurer and Tesla expect.
