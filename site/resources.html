Top 10 Tesla resources

Real, verified places to go for official service, accredited independents, charging, software tracking, battery data, safety and community — not another SEO directory.

Looking specifically for an official Tesla Service Center? See all 31 UK locations, grouped by region →

01

Tesla's official UK Service Centers Official

The authoritative list of Tesla-owned service locations — go here first if you specifically want dealer-network service.

02

The Motor Ombudsman — EV Servicing garage finder Accredited

Search by town or postcode for garages independently accredited under the EV Servicing code, with real customer reviews attached.

03

HEVRA — Hybrid and Electric Vehicle Repair Alliance Trade body

A UK-wide network of 200+ independent garages vetted and technically supported specifically for EV and hybrid repair. Owned by the Independent Garage Association. Read about it via Fully Charged's overview.

04

Zapmap Charging

The UK's leading charge point map — find nearby chargers, plan routes, and compare fuel-vs-electricity running costs.

05

Not a Tesla App Software

Tracks every Tesla software update release in detail — what's new, which regions and hardware have it. The first place most owners check after an update notification.

06

TeslaFi Battery & trip data

Third-party logging for trips, charging habits and battery state-of-health over time — deeper history than Tesla's own app provides.

07

The EV Pros Directory

A competitor directory worth knowing — it actually shows HEVRA approval and IMI qualifications per garage rather than generic star ratings.

08

GOV.UK — Check if a vehicle has been recalled Safety

The official DVSA recall checker. Enter make, model and year to see any outstanding safety recalls — genuinely useful before buying used.

09

Tesla Motors Club Community

A long-running international owner forum with deep, searchable threads on real faults, battery degradation specifics and fixes.

10

Tesla Owners UK Owners club

The official, not-for-profit UK owners club — over 10,000 members, regional meetups, and direct representation to Tesla and government on driver issues.

11

Blackcircles — book a Tesla MOT or EV service online Booking network

A national booking site for tyres, MOTs and servicing through partner garages (over 2,200 in total; they say some are certified for EV servicing). Useful for comparing prices and booking an MOT online. Check that the garage you pick is EV-trained before booking a service. Note: Tesla does not set a fixed annual service — see our before you book checklist.

12

Approved Garages — Tesla servicing and repairs Garage network

A UK network of independent garages; its Tesla section lets you search by town for members offering EV diagnostics and repairs. A second place to look if we don't list anyone close to you.
