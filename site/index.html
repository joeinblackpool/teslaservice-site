Warranty

Will an independent garage void my Tesla warranty?

Routine servicing at a non-Tesla garage generally doesn't void the vehicle warranty under UK consumer law, provided the right parts and procedures are used — but battery and drive unit work is different. Keep every invoice and ask the garage to confirm parts specification in writing.

Servicing

Does a Tesla need annual servicing at all?

Tesla doesn't require fixed-interval servicing the way petrol cars do — there's no cambelt or oil change. What matters is brake fluid moisture testing (every 2 years), tyre wear and rotation, and HVAC filtration. A specialist quoting a full "annual service" menu copied from petrol cars is a signal to ask more questions.

Choosing a garage

What should I actually ask before booking?

Ask what high-voltage EV qualification the technicians hold (IMI TechSafe Level 3 EV is the standard to look for), whether they have Tesla-specific diagnostic access, and whether they can source OEM parts or only aftermarket. A garage that answers clearly and specifically is a good sign.

Choosing

Which kind of garage is right for your Tesla?

There are now more than 200,000 Teslas on UK roads (IAAF, July 2025), and four main routes to get one serviced or repaired. Most owners use more than one over the life of the car.

Tesla Service Centre

We list 32 official Tesla Service Centres. Go here for warranty repairs, recalls, high-voltage battery and drive unit faults, and anything Tesla must diagnose with its own software. You book through the Tesla app, and some jobs are done at home by Tesla's mobile technicians.

HEVRA-accredited EV specialist

317 HEVRA-accredited garages work on electric cars. They are often the quickest and cheapest for out-of-warranty jobs: tyres, brakes, suspension, 12V batteries, wheel alignment and brake fluid checks. Ask whether their technicians hold the IMI TechSafe Level 3 EV qualification.

Independents and mobile mechanics

A further 152 independents and mobile services cover jobs such as tyres, cabin filters, alignment and diagnostics, sometimes at your home or workplace. Check what they can and can't do on a Tesla before you book.

Tesla-approved bodyshop

After an accident, an approved bodyshop (81 listed) has Tesla's repair procedures and parts access. Tell your insurer you want one, as some policies send you to their own network first.

Parts, wraps, paint protection, windscreens and detailing are in parts & cosmetic (78 listed). Not sure? Read before you book a service, or search by postcode below. Every listing links to that business's own reviews and website, so you can check them yourself.
