Official Tesla service vs an independent Tesla specialist

Both are legitimate choices. Which one you need depends on the job, and above all on whether Tesla's warranty should pay for it.

 Official Tesla service centreIndependent specialist (e.g. HEVRA-accredited)
Warranty repairsYes. Tesla's UK warranty page says that to receive warranty-covered service you must take the car to a Tesla Service Centre.No. They can't carry out work under Tesla's warranty.
Non-warranty servicing and repairsYesYes. Tesla says you may use a Tesla or a non-Tesla service centre for non-warranty work. Tesla is not responsible for problems caused by improper third-party repair or maintenance.
Recalls and software-level repairsYesUsually not. Recalls are done by Tesla.
How to bookOnly through the Tesla app (see below)Direct with the garage, by phone or its website
Opening hoursMostly Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm (see table)Varies. Many open on Saturdays.
Comes to youTesla Mobile Service can do some jobs at your home or workSome independents are mobile. Check each listing.

Sources: Tesla UK, Vehicle Warranty; Tesla UK, Schedule and Manage Service Appointments. Car-maker aftermarket agreements in the UK, including repair and maintenance, fall under the Motor Vehicle Agreements Block Exemption Order 2023, which is in force from 1 June 2023 to 31 May 2029. Our Tesla warranty UK guide covers what the warranty includes and excludes.

How to book a Tesla service (Tesla app)

Tesla's UK support page describes booking only through the app:

  1. Open the Tesla app and tap Service.
  2. Choose a support topic and describe the problem.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts. Once Tesla has finished remote diagnostics, the app notifies you to schedule the appointment.
  4. Before any work starts, review and confirm the cost estimate (Service > View estimate). Jobs that don't need a service centre may be offered as Mobile Service at an address you choose.
  5. To cancel, go to Service, find the appointment, tap Edit, then Cancel Request.

If your car won't drive, call roadside assistance on +44 (0)1628 450 660. The number is also in the car under Controls > Service. For anything outside Tesla's warranty, compare a quote from a HEVRA-accredited independent. For accident damage, see Tesla-approved body shops in the UK.

Where can I get my Tesla serviced?

Warranty and recall work goes to an official Tesla service centre. Book it in the Tesla app at any of the centres above. Routine servicing, tyres, brakes, suspension and other out-of-warranty work can go to a Tesla service centre or an independent EV specialist.
