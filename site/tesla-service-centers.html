Tesla-owned and operated locations — the only places that can do warranty work, recalls and the deepest diagnostic/software-level repairs.

Last checked: 28 September 2026. Tesla opens, closes and relocates centres — always confirm on Tesla's own locator or in the Tesla app before travelling.

Booking & contact basics: Service appointments should be booked through the Tesla app, not by phone or walk-in. For a breakdown or an immobile car, call UK roadside assistance: 01628 450 660. Tesla also runs mobile service vans for certain repairs that come to your home or workplace. Escalations after speaking with local service management go to ContactUK@tesla.com.
