Tesla Service UK / Guides / Service cost

How much does a Tesla service cost in the UK?

There is no fixed Tesla service and so no fixed price. You pay for the items on Tesla's maintenance list as they fall due, plus tyres, brakes and the MOT. Here is what that adds up to, from published UK figures and owners' own invoices.

Last fact-checked: 9 November 2026 by the XKey Developments team. Prices vary by model, car and garage, so always ask for a written quote before work starts.

The short answer

Teslas are cheap to service, but tyres are not

In Cap HPI's UK analysis of the top 20 car brands, published in May 2024, Tesla had the lowest servicing cost of any brand: £338 on average over three years and 60,000 miles1. Brakes were also the cheapest, at £113 over the same period1.

Tyres were the opposite. At £2,377 over three years and 60,000 miles, Tesla came 20th of 20, the most expensive1. That put Tesla's total service, maintenance and repair cost at £2,946, 16th of the 20 brands1. For most UK owners, tyres are the biggest running cost after electricity.

What you pay for

The jobs that make up a Tesla "service"

Tesla publishes a list of maintenance items, each with its own interval, in place of an annual service2. These are the jobs you will be quoted for:

  • Cabin air filter: every 2 years2, or 3 years on a Model Y fitted with HEPA and carbon filters3. One of the cheapest jobs, and one many owners do themselves.
  • Brake caliper clean and lubrication: every year or 12,500 miles if your roads are salted in winter2, which covers most of the UK. It takes more labour than a filter change, so it raises the bill.
  • Brake fluid: health check every 4 years, replaced if necessary2.
  • Tyre rotation: every 10,000 km (about 6,250 miles), or sooner if tread depths differ by 1.5 mm or more2. Rotating on time is the cheapest way to cut the tyre bill.
  • Wiper blades: every year2.
  • Battery coolant: under most circumstances it does not need replacing for the life of the car2.

Brake pads, tyres and the low-voltage battery are replaced when worn, not on a schedule2. See the full Tesla service schedule for when each job is due.

Real invoices

What UK owners say they paid

These are single invoices that owners posted on the Tesla Motors Club forum, not price lists. Your quote will depend on your car and the garage4:

  • £53.76: cabin filters on a Model 3 (June 2023)4.
  • £160: tyre rotation and wipers4.
  • £271.92: a year-2 filter change plus the yearly brake lubrication4. Owners in the thread put the higher price down to the brake work, which means taking the pads out to clean and grease the slide pins.

Official or independent?

Where you service a Tesla changes the price

Tesla Service Centres do warranty and recall work, and anything that needs Tesla-only tools. Routine jobs like filters, wipers, tyres, brake servicing and the MOT can also be done by an independent garage. Ideally choose a HEVRA-accredited EV specialist, whose technicians are trained to work safely on high-voltage cars. Using an independent garage for routine maintenance does not on its own void the warranty: see our Tesla warranty guide for Tesla's wording and the law.

MOT

How much is a Tesla MOT?

A Tesla needs its first MOT by the third anniversary of registration, then every year5. The test centre cannot charge more than the official maximum fee, which GOV.UK publishes for cars5. Electric cars skip the emissions test, but brakes, tyres and suspension are checked. Our Tesla MOT guide covers what Teslas most often fail on.

Save money

How to keep Tesla running costs down

  • Look after the tyres: rotate on time and check the inner edges, where heavy EVs often wear first. See Tesla tyre wear.
  • Don't skip the caliper service in winter: seized calipers and rusty discs cost far more than the lubrication. See seized brakes and rusty discs.
  • Do the easy jobs yourself: the cabin filter and wipers are owner jobs on most models. See Tesla DIY jobs ranked.
  • Check before you book: our before-you-book checklist helps you avoid paying for work you don't need.
